09:00 What it does

Your whole week on one screen

  • Your weekly timetable

    Type a course code such as CS103 and pick your section. Lecture times and rooms come from the official IUS schedule; lecturers, assistants, ECTS and office hours from eCampus.

  • Midterm dates and rooms

    The date, time and room of each of your midterms, from the official midterm schedule. During midterm week, Today shows your exams instead of classes.

  • Quizzes from classmates

    Students report upcoming quizzes, the club checks each report, and the quiz then shows for everyone in that course and section.

  • Class reminders

    On Android: a reminder before each class and an evening summary of tomorrow, each switched on in Settings.

  • 20 themes

    Light, dark, calm and fun ones, plus six living themes where bees, cats, fish and snow move around. Or let it follow your phone.

  • English, Turkish and Bosnian

    Change the language in Settings, behind the gear at the top right.

  • Works offline

    The Android app opens with no connection, and the browser version keeps its last copy too.

  • Your timetable stays on your phone

    It is stored only on your phone or in your browser and is never uploaded. No account, no login.

10:30 How it works

From the official PDF to your phone, every night

Nobody types the course list in by hand. A small program collects it every night, and your phone downloads the result.

  1. 1

    IUS schedule PDF and eCampus

    The official term schedule gives lecture times and rooms. eCampus gives syllabus links, ECTS, lecturers, tutorial and lab sections, and office hours.

  2. 2

    Nightly collector on GitHub Actions

    Every night it looks up only this term's courses, waits 10 seconds between requests as eCampus asks, and saves any changes.

  3. 3

    GitHub Pages

    Each change republishes the app's website on its own. It is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

  4. 4

    Your phone

    The app loads the newest version when you are online and keeps a copy for when you are not. Your timetable is saved only here.

Why it is built this way

  • You add your own classes

    Reading your schedule from SIS would need your SIS password. Typing a code and tapping a section takes a few seconds, and your password stays yours.

  • Two sources, each for what it does best

    The schedule PDF is the most current source for lecture times and rooms. Starting from it, the collector checks about 250 courses on eCampus instead of 1,700.

  • One polite collector, not every phone

    One program reads eCampus once a night instead of every phone asking it. If eCampus changes its pages, one fix in the collector repairs it for everyone.

  • Install once, updates arrive by themselves

    The Android app loads its screens from GitHub Pages, so fixes and new features reach everyone without a new APK.

  • Opens without internet

    The APK carries its own copy of the app, and the timetable is stored on the phone, so nothing is lost when you go offline and back.

  • One app for Android and the browser

    The Android app is a thin shell around the web app, so both run the same code, and iPhone users get the browser version.

13:30 Install

On your Android phone in three steps

  1. On your phone, open the latest release on GitHub.
  2. Download class-schedule.apk and open it.
  3. Allow installing from this source when Android asks.

You only do this once. New screens and course data arrive by themselves whenever the phone is online.

15:00 Questions

Good to know

Is this an official IUS app?

No. It is an unofficial student project by the IUS Engineering Club, not made or endorsed by IUS. When in doubt, the official schedule and your instructor have the final word.

Is my timetable uploaded anywhere?

No. Your timetable is stored only on your phone or in your browser. The app downloads course data and the quiz list, but never sends your classes anywhere. When you report a quiz, the report goes to the club for checking, and your name is optional.

Is there an iPhone app?

No, but the browser version works on iPhone, and you can add it to your Home Screen so it opens like an app: see the five steps. Class reminders are on Android only.

A time or room is wrong. What now?

eCampus is sometimes behind; a tutorial can move before the syllabus is updated. Edit the class in Courses: times you enter yourself always win. Then tell the club at iusengclub@gmail.com so it gets fixed for everyone.

Does it cost anything?

No. It is free, and its source code is on GitHub.
